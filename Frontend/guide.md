# Pandav Design System: Visual Foundation & Style Guide

## 1. Creative North Star: "The Digital Atrium"
The Pandav design system is built around the concept of a **Digital Atrium**—a spacious, light-filled, and organized environment that fosters collaboration without the clutter of traditional enterprise software.

---

## 2. Color Palette
The palette is designed to be professional, trustworthy, and calm, using deep blues as the anchor and subtle tonal shifts for depth.

### Primary Colors
*   **Atrium Blue (Primary):** `#1A4F8B`
    *   *Usage:* Buttons, active states, key branding elements.
*   **Deep Slate (Text/Neutral):** `#0F172A`
    *   *Usage:* Main headings, primary text.
*   **Cloud White (Surface):** `#FFFFFF`
    *   *Usage:* Primary background, card surfaces.

### Functional Colors
*   **Success (Active/Regular):** `#10B981` (Emerald)
*   **Warning (Pending/Busy):** `#F59E0B` (Amber)
*   **Error (Reject/Critical):** `#EF4444` (Red)
*   **Info (Remote/New):** `#3B82F6` (Blue)

---

## 3. Typography Guide
We prioritize legibility and a modern, architectural feel.

### Primary Font: **Manrope**
*   **Headlines (Bold/Semi-bold):** Used for page titles and section headers. High tracking-tight (`-0.02em`) for a modern look.
*   **Body (Medium/Regular):** Used for navigation, labels, and paragraph text. 

### Scale
*   **Display:** 32px - 40px (Bold)
*   **Heading:** 20px - 24px (Semi-bold)
*   **Body (Default):** 14px - 16px (Medium)
*   **Labels/Captions:** 12px (Semi-bold, often Uppercase for metadata)

---

## 4. Visual Components & Layout
*   **Roundness:** `ROUND_FOUR` (4px - 8px radius) across buttons, inputs, and cards to maintain a precise, professional look.
*   **Elevation:** Flat design with subtle borders (`1px`) and tonal shifts rather than heavy shadows.
*   **Spacing:** Generous whitespace (The "Atrium" effect) with a base 8px grid.

---

## 5. Core Components Design
*   **Navigation:** A permanent side rail for deep hierarchy and a floating, translucent top bar for breadcrumbs and quick actions.
*   **Cards:** Clean, bordered containers with subtle backgrounds (`slate-50`) to separate content without adding visual weight.
*   **Inputs:** Minimalist style with light gray backgrounds and clear focus states using the Primary Atrium Blue.
